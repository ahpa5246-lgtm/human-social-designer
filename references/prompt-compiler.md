# Prompt Compiler

Compile the selected concept into one focused image instruction. Expansion must add visible precision, not source facts or decorative inventory.

## Compilation order

1. **Message** — the pivotal sentence the viewer should understand.
2. **Metaphor** — the one concrete physical relationship that embodies it.
3. **Subject and action** — exact identity, count, role, and shared action.
4. **Composition** — focal hierarchy, scale, viewpoint, crop, negative space, and safe zone.
5. **Style DNA** — medium, form, surface, edges, depth, light, palette, and detail rhythm.
6. **Text and brand** — exact minimal wording, placement behavior, and supplied logo handling.
7. **Output** — aspect ratio, intended placement, and crop constraints.
8. **Avoid** — only brief-specific failures most likely to contaminate the result.

Meaning comes before rendering. Do not begin with “beautiful 3D poster” or a style adjective pile.

## Compact template

> Create one [format] key visual communicating: [MESSAGE]. Express it as [METAPHOR]. Show [SUBJECT COUNT/IDENTITY] [ACTION/RELATIONSHIP] in one coherent scene. Composition: [VIEWPOINT, HERO SCALE, CROP, HIERARCHY, NEGATIVE SPACE]. Style DNA: [MEDIUM, FORM, SURFACE, EDGES, DEPTH, LIGHT, PALETTE, DETAIL RHYTHM]. Visible text: “[EXACT SHORT TEXT]” [INTEGRATION], with [BRAND ASSET INSTRUCTIONS]. Output: [RATIO/PLACEMENT]. Avoid: [2–5 BRIEF-SPECIFIC FAILURES].

Use one coherent paragraph when the generator responds poorly to labeled blocks.

## Compression rules

- Preserve factual identity, required count, relationships, exact short text, brand assets, and explicit exclusions.
- Omit background facts, eligibility, benefits, dates, links, bullets, and calls to action unless explicitly required in-image.
- Use concrete nouns and spatial verbs instead of praise words.
- Do not expose rejected concepts, internal evaluation, or the full source post.
- Do not prescribe every coordinate or decorative detail. Protect the concept and invariants while leaving rendering latitude.
- Use one primary visual mechanism and one dominant design lever.
- Keep the avoid list short; excessive negatives dilute the central instruction.

## Preflight

Before generation, confirm:

- the metaphor is understandable without the headline;
- subject identity, count, and action are unambiguous;
- the composition supports the requested crop;
- Style DNA describes observable rendering traits;
- typography is exact and minimal;
- the prompt contains only the current image, not multiple states or concepts;
- removing any sentence would not improve focus.

## Repair prompts

For an execution defect, preserve successful regions explicitly and correct only the failed property. Example structure:

> Preserve the accepted concept, composition, crop, hero, palette, and all unmentioned details. Correct only [DEFECT] by [VISIBLE TARGET].

For a concept defect, do not write a repair prompt. Return to concept selection and compile a genuinely new physical relationship.
