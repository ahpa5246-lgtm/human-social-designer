# Influences and License Boundaries

Human Social Designer v9 was informed by publicly documented workflows from these projects. Their ideas were adapted into an original, social-key-visual-specific system rather than copied as bundled dependencies.

## story-picture

- Repository: https://github.com/songxiaopeng529/story-picture
- License: MIT at the time reviewed.
- Influence: meaning-first article interpretation, reusable style traits, structured prompt ordering, and inspection after generation.

## art-direct-imagegen-plugin

- Repository: https://github.com/akenoowww/art-direct-imagegen-plugin
- License: MIT at the time reviewed.
- Influence: separating invariants from creative freedom, interrupting obvious answers, diverging by conceptual mechanism, selecting before prompting, and distinguishing execution defects from concept defects.

## Hunyuan PromptEnhancer

- Repository: https://github.com/Hunyuan-PromptEnhancer/PromptEnhancer
- License: Tencent Hunyuan Community License for its distributed materials at the time reviewed.
- Influence: the general principle that a short intent can be rewritten into a clearer, structured, image-oriented instruction.

No PromptEnhancer model weights, inference code, outputs, or licensed documentation text are copied, distributed, or required by this skill. Human Social Designer does not invoke or bundle the Tencent model. Its prompt compiler is an independently written rule set built from general prompt-engineering principles and the needs of this skill.

## Maintenance rule

Preserve attribution when redistributing this repository. Before importing code, model artifacts, templates, or substantial text from any upstream project, re-check its current license and add the required notices. Conceptual influence alone is not permission to copy restricted materials.
