# Arabic Typography Rules

Arabic typography is not a caption layer. It is part of the composition.

## 1. Compress before styling

A beautiful headline is usually short.

Prefer:
- 2–6 words
- one dominant phrase
- one optional support line

Avoid generated paragraphs inside images.

## 2. Build hierarchy with phrase structure

Split the headline where meaning naturally changes.

Example:

`الوقت كسيف`
`إن لم تقطعه قطعك`

The first line can be the hook, the second the consequence.

Do not break Arabic purely to fill a rectangle.

## 3. Choose one typographic behavior

Possible behaviors:
- oversized display word
- stacked phrase
- one accent word in a second color
- one word outlined, the rest solid
- partial occlusion behind the hero
- underline / rule extending into the scene
- one cropped word at the edge
- typography inside negative space

Do not combine all behaviors in one poster.

## 4. Arabic must look intentional

Prioritize:
- correct connected forms
- correct punctuation direction
- natural line breaks
- generous leading
- enough side bearing around the block
- consistent baseline logic

Avoid awkward mixed direction with Latin text.

## 5. Arabic + English

Choose a primary language.

If Arabic is primary:
- Arabic headline dominates
- English title or microcopy stays small
- English can serve as metadata texture only when meaningful

If English is primary:
- Arabic support should not fight for equal scale unless requested

## 6. Type and image relationship

Good relationships:
- object overlaps 5–15% of the headline
- headline sits in intentional negative space
- visual line continues from a letter or rule
- one word physically interacts with the concept

Bad relationship:
- headline randomly centered because space remained
- text covers important hero detail
- multiple unrelated boxes behind text

## 7. Avoid fake Arabic display

Do not distort letterforms into unreadable shapes just to appear artistic.

A headline should remain recognizable at phone-feed size.

## 8. Text density target

Default conceptual poster:
- headline: 1
- support line: 0–1
- brand mark: 0–1
- CTA/date: 0–1

Anything beyond that requires explicit justification.

## 9. Useful headline qualities

Strong social headlines are:
- concrete
- rhythmic
- surprising
- short
- emotionally clear

Prefer a phrase that adds a second layer to the image rather than restating what is visible.

## 10. Final Arabic typography check

Before generating, ask:
- Is this the shortest version that still works?
- Is the line break natural?
- Is one word doing most of the visual work?
- Is the type block shaped intentionally?
- Would the headline still feel strong without effects?
